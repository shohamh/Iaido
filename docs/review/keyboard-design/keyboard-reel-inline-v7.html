HOW TO EXPLORE0 WORDS

Surface and language

Switch the palette or key legends; Hebrew labels change in place, without mirroring the physical key rows.

Move between words

1Type freely in the message field or tap the on-screen keys. Words are generated from your text.
2Place the caret. Tap a character or the gap between words, or hold a word to place the caret at that letter and drag it through the sentence. Swipe left or right on empty strip space to scroll without moving the caret.
3Swap or delete. Swipe up or down to preview a swap. Move into another word to start deleting; cross each word center to include it. Hold at an edge while moving the caret or deleting to keep scrolling through the sentence. Scrolling speeds up deeper in the edge zone. Return to the starting word to resume its alternatives, or pull back to shrink the delete range, then release to commit.

Sentence reels

A sentence-first reel: faint words above and below each committed word. Swipe to preview the edited sentence and release to swap. Use the undo and redo arrows above the sentence; hold an enabled arrow to preview the change before releasing. Includes the “alot” → “a lot” split and “in to” → “into” join.
